HOW TO PLAY

  • Every shift runs exactly 7 minutes, from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM. Survive until the clock hits 6:00 AM.
  • Look around with A/D, the arrow keys, or by clicking the screen edges. Each station has a center desk, a left wall and a right wall.
  • Use the buttons along the bottom for the station you face. You can also click the 3D objects. ESC or || PAUSE freezes everything.
  • Red buttons mean something is active there. Amber means a toggle is on or it is a decoy.
  • Every monster has a warning sign and a counter. Learn the pattern and handle the closest threat first.

CONTROLS: Center: TV channel 1-4, DEGAUSS x3 (G), reel speed buttons (Z/X for A, C/V for B), PERISCOPE (P) and scope left/right (Q/E). Left: patchbay jacks. Right: pump each of the four doors.

The Archivist
What it does: Detunes the tape reels so their speeds drift.
How to survive: Keep BOTH reels near 1.00x with the speed buttons. Drifting outside 0.75 to 1.25 fills its meter, steady tape drains it.
The Broadcast Host
What it does: A 1970s announcer appears on one TV channel. He drains stability and pulls your camera.
How to survive: Flip through the TV channels to find the one showing him, then press DEGAUSS three times within 2 seconds. Degaussing on the wrong channel makes it worse.
The Tunnel Stalker
What it does: Comes down one of four tunnels (N/E/S/W) toward that tunnel's blast door.
How to survive: Use the periscope and rotate the scope to find his tunnel. Pump THAT door to 80% (it bleeds slowly). Sealed, he retreats after 4 seconds. Unsealed for 5 seconds and he gets in.
Patient Zero
What it does: Jumps across the patchbay one column at a time. Steady red jacks are real, flickering ones are decoys.
How to survive: Unplug the steady jacks. That removes it and every jack after it. Unplugging a decoy lets it jump ahead. Four columns and the loop closes.
