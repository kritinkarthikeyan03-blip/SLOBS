HOW TO PLAY

Look around with A/D, the arrow keys, or the screen edges. Use the buttons along the bottom for the station you face. ESC or || PAUSE freezes everything.

Unlocks after you survive Night 5 of all six chapters. One 7-minute shift. Drain the Architect's Loop Integrity to 0% before 6:00 AM. Threats appear on the board at the top of the screen, up to 2, 3 then 4 at once as the phases speed up. Each has a timer.

The Anchor (CENTER): switch camera. Depth Echo (LEFT): sonar pulse. The Echo (RIGHT): compare the code with the codebook strip, Accept real, Purge fake. Patient Zero (RIGHT): unplug the jack shown (keys 1-6). The Chorus (CENTER): set the polarity shown. The Mariner (LEFT): rotate the lamp onto the bearing and hold it.

Every cleared threat damages the boss (chain clears for combo damage). A missed threat costs 25% Stability, a wrong jack 4%. Lose all Stability or run out of time and the loop restarts.
