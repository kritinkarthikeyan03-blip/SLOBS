HOW TO PLAY

  • Every shift runs exactly 7 minutes, from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM. Survive until the clock hits 6:00 AM.
  • Look around with A/D, the arrow keys, or by clicking the screen edges. Each station has a center desk, a left wall and a right wall.
  • Use the buttons along the bottom for the station you face. You can also click the 3D objects. ESC or || PAUSE freezes everything.
  • Red buttons mean something is active there. Amber means a toggle is on or it is a decoy.
  • Every monster has a warning sign and a counter. Learn the pattern and handle the closest threat first.

CONTROLS: Center: the 5x5 packet grid, ACCEPT (Y), PURGE (N). Left: close or open the cupola panels LEFT/CENTER/RIGHT (Z/X/C). Right: duct gates 1-3 and the DUCT PURGE (G).

The Phantom Cosmonaut
What it does: Knocks on the cupola glass from one of three segments. The knocks are stereo-panned.
How to survive: Listen for the side the knocking comes from and close that panel. He leaves after 2 seconds. Leave it open through 7 seconds of knocking and he gets in. Closed panels drain reserve power.
The Solar Parasite
What it does: A flare corrupts the telemetry grid. Cells flash red (corrupt) or amber (decoy).
How to survive: Click red cells before they expire. Never click amber. Clear 10 to end it. Missed red cells fill the corruption meter.
The Echo
What it does: Houston calls with a 4-digit hex code. About half are fakes, one character off a valid code. The valid codes change every hour.
How to survive: Check the current hour in the codebook strip at the top. Accept real, purge fake. Accepting a fake or ignoring one kills you. Purging a real call locks comms for 8 seconds.
The Hull Crawler
What it does: Crawls through a duct graph toward your chair, preferring forward routes.
How to survive: Close gates to block routes and steer it into dead ends. A gate closed on it pushes it back. Closed gates raise CO2, and it gnaws a gate open after about 6 seconds. DUCT PURGE sends it back to the start for 25% CO2.
