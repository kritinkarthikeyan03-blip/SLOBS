HOW TO PLAY

  • Every shift runs exactly 7 minutes, from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM. Survive until the clock hits 6:00 AM.
  • Look around with A/D, the arrow keys, or by clicking the screen edges. Each station has a center desk, a left wall and a right wall.
  • Use the buttons along the bottom for the station you face. You can also click the 3D objects. ESC or || PAUSE freezes everything.
  • Red buttons mean something is active there. Amber means a toggle is on or it is a decoy.
  • Every monster has a warning sign and a counter. Learn the pattern and handle the closest threat first.

CONTROLS: Left: rotate the lamp (Q/E) and strike matches (R). Center: short and long foghorn (Z/X). Right: close inlets (keys 1-3), pump (Space), refill oil (O).

The Drowned Mariner
What it does: A drowned sailor walks the horizon. The radar shows his bearing and he keeps moving.
How to survive: Rotate the lamp to keep the beam within 15 degrees of him for about 2 seconds. In the dark he gains twice as fast.
The Foghorn Chorus
What it does: A ghostly horn plays 3 to 5 blasts. At higher levels the pattern is hidden after 2.5 seconds.
How to survive: Repeat the blast pattern exactly with SHORT and LONG. A wrong blast resets your input and speeds it up.
The Tide Wraith
What it does: Floods the cellar through glowing inlets on the right.
How to survive: Close the glowing (red) inlets first, then pump the bilge. Pumping with an inlet open barely helps. At 100% you drown.
The Wick Eater
What it does: Snuffs the lamp flame. Fifteen seconds of darkness is fatal.
How to survive: Strike a match when the meter is in the green band and there is no GUST. Two good strikes relight it. You only have 5 matches. Refill oil with O. Oil only drains while the lamp burns.
