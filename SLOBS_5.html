HOW TO PLAY

  • Every shift runs exactly 7 minutes, from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM. Survive until the clock hits 6:00 AM.
  • Look around with A/D, the arrow keys, or by clicking the screen edges. Each station has a center desk, a left wall and a right wall.
  • Use the buttons along the bottom for the station you face. You can also click the 3D objects. ESC or || PAUSE freezes everything.
  • Red buttons mean something is active there. Amber means a toggle is on or it is a decoy.
  • Every monster has a warning sign and a counter. Learn the pattern and handle the closest threat first.

CONTROLS: Center: four voice-polarity switches (keys 1-4). Left: six roof panels (U I O J K L) and the THERMAL ARC (T). Right: dump capacitors A/B/C (G/H/Y).

The Chorus
What it does: Four voices demand a polarity pattern, and the pattern flips one bit every 7 seconds.
How to survive: Keep your four switches matching the CHORUS pattern shown on the top bar. The more bits wrong, the faster its meter rises.
The Borealis Wraith
What it does: Hovers over one of six roof panels (highlighted) and floods the dome with radiation under it. It drifts to a neighbouring panel every 6 seconds.
How to survive: Close the highlighted panel. Panels overheat and reopen after 9 seconds, then cool down for 6, so keep chasing it. Closed panels drain reserve.
The Frostbitten Signalman
What it does: Prowls outside the dome. Noise and movement raise his detection.
How to survive: While the note says he is prowling, stay still: do not swivel or press buttons. If detection hits 100% he pounds the airlock, so fire the THERMAL ARC to drive him off (it works while he prowls too).
The Singularity Core
What it does: Three capacitors charge at different speeds.
How to survive: Dump each capacitor once it is over 35% (wasted below that). Dumping between 70% and 95% also vents the others by 15%. Any at 100% means meltdown.
