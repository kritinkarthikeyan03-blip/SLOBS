HOW TO PLAY

  • Every shift runs exactly 7 minutes, from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM. Survive until the clock hits 6:00 AM.
  • Look around with A/D, the arrow keys, or by clicking the screen edges. Each station has a center desk, a left wall and a right wall.
  • Use the buttons along the bottom for the station you face. You can also click the 3D objects. ESC or || PAUSE freezes everything.
  • Red buttons mean something is active there. Amber means a toggle is on or it is a decoy.
  • Every monster has a warning sign and a counter. Learn the pattern and handle the closest threat first.

CONTROLS: Center: DISH left/right (Q/E), PULSE (P), KILL-SWITCH (K). Left: SEAL the bulkhead (S). Right: STOP PIPE 1-3 (keys 1-3), O2 CAN (O). Almost everything you do makes noise.

The Depth Mimic
What it does: A fake ping with a red double echo creeps toward the center from one bearing.
How to survive: Rotate the dish so the green cone covers the double echo (within 25 degrees) and pulse. You have 3 charges and one regenerates every 12 seconds. A miss still makes noise.
The Barnacle Swarm
What it does: Foul a ballast pipe. Each fouled pipe raises hull pressure. At 100% the viewport cracks.
How to survive: On the right panel a needle spins around each fouled gauge. Press STOP when it is inside the green arc. A miss speeds the needle up and locks the button briefly.
The Diver
What it does: Walks to your hatch. Noise makes him bang harder.
How to survive: Seal the bulkhead when he arrives, then stay quiet (noise under 8%) for 3 seconds and he leaves. Sealing drains oxygen fast, so use the O2 cans.
The Signal Leviathan
What it does: A proximity spike warns that it is near.
How to survive: Hit the kill-switch within 6 seconds, then stay silent for the whole 9-second pass. Noise above 30% or turning the lights back on gets you killed. Never pulse while it is near.
