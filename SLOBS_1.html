HOW TO PLAY

  • Every shift runs exactly 7 minutes, from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM. Survive until the clock hits 6:00 AM.
  • Look around with A/D, the arrow keys, or by clicking the screen edges. Each station has a center desk, a left wall and a right wall.
  • Use the buttons along the bottom for the station you face. You can also click the 3D objects. ESC or || PAUSE freezes everything.
  • Red buttons mean something is active there. Amber means a toggle is on or it is a decoy.
  • Every monster has a warning sign and a counter. Learn the pattern and handle the closest threat first.

CONTROLS: Center: CAM 1-4 buttons (keys 1-4), MONITOR (M), FLASH (F), tuner COARSE/FINE (J/L and I/K), DECOY (V). Left wall: SHUT/OPEN the steel door (C). Right wall: the 3x3 fuse grid and the generator crank.

The Anchor
What it does: A weeping-angel stalker. It moves through four cameras (Roof, Stairs, Hall, Desk) only while you are NOT watching its camera. At the Desk it climbs out of the monitor.
How to survive: Find which camera it is on, then keep that camera up so it freezes. FLASH while watching it to shove it back a step (10s cooldown, 4% power). Turning the monitor off makes it move faster.
The Whisperer
What it does: Hijacks the scope. Two tones play, the target and yours. When they are close you hear a slow beating.
How to survive: Use COARSE and FINE tuning until the beat slows and stops, then hold it for 2.5 seconds. Listen, do not guess.
The Lineman
What it does: Walks Roof, Stairs, Hall, then knocks at the door: three knocks, then he tests the handle 0.6 to 1.8 seconds after the third.
How to survive: Shut the door right after knock 3 and keep it shut until he leaves. Door stamina drains while shut, so do not hold it early. Decoys (4 per night) push him back two steps.
The Overloader
What it does: Scrambles the 3x3 fuse grid. Faults drain signal integrity. At zero (or 0% power) you black out.
How to survive: It is a lights-out puzzle: clicking a fuse flips it and its four neighbours. Turn every fuse green. In a blackout, crank the generator to 100 within 9 seconds.
